{\large \textbf{Research}}
\vspace{0.15cm}\\
\textbf{VisConf: Quality-Aware Test-Time Scaling for VLMs} \hfill NeurIPS 2026 Workshop | 2026
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep, topsep=0pt, partopsep=0pt, parsep=0pt]
    \item Developed \textbf{VisConf}, a rollout-quality score for Best-of-N inference that combines \textbf{visual groundedness}, measured from attention to image tokens, with \textbf{model confidence} measured by $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(U\|p)$ over the output distribution.
    \item Introduced \textbf{Rank-Weighted Consensus (RWC)}, which weights answer votes by rollout quality so that the final answer across up to \textbf{32 rollouts} is chosen by quality-aware voting rather than frequency alone. The method is verifier-free and training-free, and was evaluated on \textbf{Qwen2.5-VL-3B} and \textbf{InternVL3-8B} across MathVerse, MathVista, MMMU-Pro and MMStar.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Mechanistic Interpretability for Post-Translational Modification Prediction} \hfill In progress
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep, topsep=0pt, partopsep=0pt, parsep=0pt]
    \item Reproducing \textbf{SAPP} and \textbf{GraphPhos} as baselines and building a pipeline that predicts PTM sites from protein sequence and residue-level structure, replacing summaries based only on relative solvent accessibility (RSA) with contact graphs and confidence-aware geometry.
    \item Investigating the \textbf{mechanistic basis} of why these methods perform well, in order to identify interpretable architectural signals and design more accurate and robust PTM predictors.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Open-Set Recognition in Federated Learning} \hfill In progress
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep, topsep=0pt, partopsep=0pt, parsep=0pt]
    \item Developed latent-space augmentations that generate \textbf{synthetic negatives}, and a KL-divergence regularizer that encourages them to have equal affinity to all class prototypes, improving separation from known classes.
    \item Formulating a \textbf{discriminative-subspace hypothesis} to explain why particular augmentations improve open-set recognition and which representation directions matter most under federated heterogeneity.
\end{itemize}
